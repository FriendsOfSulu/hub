Adds configurable bulk actions to Sulu Admin lists, with built-in support for publishing, unpublishing, copying locales and deleting articles, snippets, testimonials and events on the 3.x branch. Custom resources can provide their own handlers, and permissions are checked for each entry. Use the 3.x branch for Sulu 3 or the 2.x branch for Sulu 2.6; the latter declares a Composer requirement of ^2.5.
