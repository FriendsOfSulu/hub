Provides appointment management in Sulu with a FullCalendar admin interface offering month, week and day views. Resources such as doctors, rooms and devices support collision detection, split business hours and an availability wizard for finding suitable time slots. Includes multilingual content, sub-appointments with individual resources and statuses, and a status audit log.
